Why The Week Looks Like This
  • Monday is the heaviest day — it's your only gym day not followed by serious field work (Tuesday's 7v7 is casual).
  • Wed/Fri mornings are upper-only — pressing and pulling won't steal from afternoon training legs. Lift ends 4+ hours before training.
  • Thursday is RPE-capped — heavy enough to drive the hinge weakness, controlled enough that Saturday's match legs survive. Never chase a PR here.
  • The cardio system is polarized — mostly easy, occasionally hard, nothing in between. Zone 2 (Tue 30', Wed 20') builds the engine with zero interference to strength or soreness cost. The single HIIT dose (Mon) raises the ceiling and sits 5 days from Saturday. Scrimmage intensity is left as-is — it's skill practice, not conditioning. Fri–Sun is a hard taper: fresh legs are programmed, not hoped for.
  • Plyos + GK work are structured, not skipped — since team sessions are scrimmage-heavy, the isolated jump/dive/reaction work lives here: Monday power block (~35 contacts), Thursday lateral dose (~24 contacts), Wednesday hands block, and the 15-min GK Primer before Wed/Fri training. Total weekly jump contacts stay ≤ ~70 while the ankle rebuilds.
Operating Rules
  • Progression: when you hit all target reps with 2 in reserve, add 10 lb (lower) or 5 lb (upper) next time — then tap the chip and update your target.
  • Blocks, not weekly rotation: run Block 1 for 4 straight weeks, adding load each week — repeating the same lifts weekly is what drives strength and technique, especially on your rebuilding lifts. Deload week 5, then switch to Block 2 for weeks 6–9, deload week 10, retest InBody. Fixed-tagged slots never change across blocks.
  • Deload: week 5 and week 10 (the block boundaries) — half the sets at the same weights, skip Monday sprints. If you played 3+ matches in a week, pull the deload forward.
  • Time-cap rule: running long? Cut in this order: Optional-tagged → D items → C items. Never cut A/B compounds or orange rehab.
  • Cardio rules: always bike over running while the ankle rebuilds. Zone 2 means conversational — going "medium" on easy days is the classic way to wreck both recovery and the strength work. If a lift session must be cut short, the barbell work survives and the spin dies.
  • Match-week adjustment: tournament weekend coming? Skip Thursday's barbell work, keep the rehab + core only.
  • Red flags: sharp quad pain on eccentrics, ankle giving way on landings, or swelling next morning → stop loading, see a physio. General guidance here isn't a substitute for one.
  • Re-test: InBody at 10–12 weeks. Success = legs ≥115% of ideal, L/R leg gap under 2 points, weight stable or slightly up.
Fuel note: With this combined gym + field load you're likely burning 3,000+ kcal on double days. Eating at your old maintenance will slowly dig a hole — target ~2,800–3,000 kcal and 140–175 g protein on training days.
