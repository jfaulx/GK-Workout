5 min warm-up
4 min HARD
3 min easy
4 rounds
Effort: RPE 8.5–9, HR reaching ~160–170 (you hit 180 max in September, so this is ~90–95%). Hold the same power every round.
Why: 4 × 4 is the best-evidenced protocol for lifting VO2 max. It is hard, so keep your sleep and food in order around it.

3 sets, 4 min between

Why: this matches how goalkeeping actually taxes you: short bursts with incomplete recovery. It sharpens you for the season.
